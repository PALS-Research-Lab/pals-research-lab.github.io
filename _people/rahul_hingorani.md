  Rahul is joining the PALS Lab as a sabbatical researcher from the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory (APL). At the PALS Lab, Rahul will apply these multimodal analysis approaches to better characterize individual differences in the biological, behavioral, and clinical manifestations of mood disorders, with the goal of advancing more personalized approaches to mental health care.
