Ananya Joshi is an assistant professor of psychiatry and behavioral sciences at the Johns Hopkins
University School of Medicine, with a joint appointment in the Whiting School of Engineering's
Department of Computer Science and an affiliate appointment at the Malone Center for Engineering
in Healthcare. She researches computational and generative AI methods for diagnosing, treating,
and monitoring mood disorders.

She received her PhD and MS in computer science from Carnegie Mellon University, where she was
supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and developed AI methods for data monitoring.
