Research

We work at the intersection of psychiatry and computer science, building monitoring methods, AI systems for clinical processes, and rigorous ways to evaluate GenAI in behavioral health. Our work is inspired by the mission of Johns Hopkins Medicine to "improve the health of the community and the world by setting the standard of excellence in medical education, research and clinical care."
