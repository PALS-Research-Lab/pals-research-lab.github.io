Psychiatry-Assistive Learning Systems

We conduct research at the frontiers of psychiatry and computer science, building functional AI systems for improved patient care and advancing ML methods through the lens of clinical psychiatry.

Research People

Our Mission

Psychiatric care depends on evidence that arrives in time to act on. We build AI systems and statistical methods that make that evidence available to patients, clinicians, and hospitals, and we hold those systems to clinical standards of rigor before they reach anyone's care.

That means designing methods alongside the clinicians who would use them, and treating evaluation as part of the research rather than an afterthought.
